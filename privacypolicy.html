Privacy Policy

This policy describes what ChestPlayer collects, why it is needed, and how it is protected.

Important: This is the product privacy notice for the current ChestPlayer build. The operator must review it with privacy counsel, add its legal entity and contact details, confirm retention periods and regional rights, and publish any required jurisdiction-specific notices before launch.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to ChestPlayer websites, game rooms, account tools, payout operations, customer support, and related services that link to it. “ChestPlayer,” “we,” and “us” mean the service operator identified in the final launch contact information.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and wallet data: Supabase authentication identifiers, wallet identity data, linked EVM wallet address, network, sign-in events, and account settings.
  • Gameplay and ledger data: match entries, answers, timestamps, scores, room events, balance movements, deposits, withdrawals, payout decisions, tie splits, and promotion credits.
  • Identity-review data: legal name, date of birth, country, address, selfie, photo ID showing address, document hashes, review status, reviewer notes, and review history.
  • Recording data: camera and microphone media captured during consented paid games, recording metadata, upload hashes, access events, and deletion events.
  • Security and support data: request IDs, browser and device information, locale, error events, rate-limit signals, and privacy-preserving security fingerprints used to detect abuse. The application should not place private keys, cashout codes, raw KYC documents, or raw media in logs.

3. Why we use information

We use information to authenticate users, link and protect payout destinations, operate and settle matches, verify deposits, prevent fraud and cheating, conduct manual KYC and payout review, answer support requests, maintain audit trails, secure the service, provide localized questions, record every paid 10-10 Flash Trivia and 5x5 ChestPlayer game, administer gifts, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Where required, we rely on consent for camera, microphone, and identity-document processing; you can decline, but paid gameplay or payout approval may then be unavailable.

4. Sharing and service providers

We share only what is needed with service providers that operate the product, such as Supabase for authentication, database, and private storage; Cloudflare for Worker execution, delivery, rate limiting, and email routing; GitHub Pages for the static public site; blockchain networks and indexing services for transaction verification; and the operator’s payout, support, and professional advisers. KYC evidence and recordings are restricted to authorized review personnel and approved service infrastructure. We do not sell private KYC documents or game recordings.

5. Retention

We retain account, ledger, payout, audit, and security records for as long as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal or accounting requirements. Recordings from every paid 10-10 and 5x5 game are private and scheduled for deletion after 30 days unless a longer hold is needed for an open review, dispute, fraud investigation, or legal obligation. KYC documents are retained only as long as needed for the review, security, dispute, and compliance purposes disclosed by the operator, then deleted or rendered inaccessible according to the deletion workflow.

6. Security

ChestPlayer uses server-authoritative game state, row-level access controls, private storage, signed short-lived media URLs, service-side validation, hashed credential checks, immutable payout-wallet snapshots, idempotency controls, append-only review records, and audit logging. No online system is risk-free. Keep your wallet, cashout code, and security answer private and report suspicious signature requests immediately.

7. Cookies and local storage

The public interface may use browser storage for locale, display preferences, and a temporary session handled by Supabase. The Worker uses necessary request metadata and security controls. Review your browser’s storage and permission controls if you do not want to grant camera or microphone access.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Some records may need to be retained to protect the ledger, investigate abuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Send requests through the operator’s monitored privacy contact published at launch; identity verification may be required to protect the account.

9. Children and international use

ChestPlayer is not intended for anyone under 18. Do not create an account if you are under the applicable minimum age. The service may process information across countries through its infrastructure providers. The operator must provide any required transfer safeguards and regional supplements before launch.

10. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the service or processing changes. The published version date appears above, and the onboarding record stores the Privacy Policy version accepted by the user. Before launch, the operator should replace this paragraph with its legal name, mailing address, monitored privacy email, and any required data-protection officer contact.
