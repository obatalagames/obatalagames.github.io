3. Matches and answers

10-10 Flash Trivia

Flash is a 1-on-1, ten-question, multiple-choice match. The first player receives a ten-second attempt. A wrong answer or timeout passes the same question to the opponent for a ten-second attempt. Scores, clocks, question selection, and settlement are determined by the server.

Scheduled matches

Scheduled matches are called 5x5 ChestPlayer in the game interface. Each room has five players, a $100 USDT entry, and a 25-clue write-in board across five categories. Each attempt has a ten-second limit. The room starts only when all five seats and all required recording consents are present. If the room does not meet its start conditions by the cutoff, it is cancelled and reserved entries are refunded once.

Answer evaluation and ties

Answers are evaluated against the published question answer and approved aliases. Matching is case-insensitive and normalizes punctuation and spacing; approved equivalents such as “mise-en-place” and “mise en place” may be treated as the same answer. A tie in a settled scheduled room splits the applicable prize pool evenly among the tied players, subject to the ledger’s smallest-unit rounding rules.
