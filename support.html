PLAYER SUPPORT

Need a hand?

Tell us what happened and include the time, page, wallet address, and visible request ID when one is shown. Never send a private key, seed phrase, cashout code, security answer, or identity document by email.

SIGN-IN

Wallet sign-in

Open ChestPlayer directly at chestplayer.us, unlock your wallet, and approve the message only if it names ChestPlayer. If your wallet was signed for another site, close the request and start again from the home page.

MATCHES

Game or connection issue

Keep the match page open, note the clock and the request ID, and do not refresh repeatedly. The server remains the source of truth for the question, turn, score, and result.

DEPOSITS

Deposit question

Use only the current address and token shown in your signed-in dashboard. Blockchain transfers cannot be reversed by the site. Include the transaction hash when you contact support.

PAYOUTS

Withdrawal question

Payouts are reviewed manually and can only go to your saved wallet. Your KYC documents are submitted inside the private dashboard flow; do not attach them to support email.

CONTACT

Contact the support team

Send a short description from the email address associated with your support request. Please include a timestamp and request ID, but keep secrets and identity documents out of the message.
