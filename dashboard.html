PLAY GUIDE · RULES AT A GLANCE

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before joining a room, choosing a clue, or requesting a payout.

READ FIRST
?
Short-answer trivia with a simple clock.

Every clue is designed for a one-, two-, or three-word answer. You see the clue, the timer, and your score as you play.

10-10 FLASH

How does Flash Trivia work?

It is a 1-on-1 match with 10 lower-difficulty random questions. The active player gets one 10-second try. If the answer is wrong or the clock expires, that question is skipped and the correct answer is not displayed.

ACCOUNT

How do I sign in or create an account?

Select Sign in / Sign up and connect MetaMask. MetaMask approves the sign-in, and your private key stays in your wallet.

SCHEDULED MATCHES

What is 5x5 ChestPlayer?

Scheduled matches have exactly five players, a fixed $100 entry, and a 25-clue Jeopardy-style grid: five categories by five values. At launch, one match is offered every two hours in Central Time.

10 SECONDS

What happens on the board?

The selector chooses an available tile. The selector gets the first 10-second attempt. A wrong answer or timeout passes the same clue to the next seat, up to all five players. A correct answer earns the tile value and selection rotates.

ANSWERS

How are answers matched?

Answers are not case-sensitive. Punctuation, hyphens, accents, and repeated spaces are handled for you, and approved synonyms can be accepted.

START GATE

What if a scheduled match is not full?

The match starts only with all five seats filled at the scheduled time. If fewer than five users sign up by the cutoff, it is cancelled and every reserved entry is refunded exactly once.

TIES

How are ties settled?

Players tied for the top score share the prize pool equally. A three-way tie receives one-third per player.

MONEY

What are the entry and payout rules?

Flash entries are $5–$50 USDT; scheduled ChestPlayer entries are $100. Payout requests use your saved cashout code and go only to your saved wallet. Every payout requires a selfie, photo ID showing address, and a quick identity review.

DAILY GIFT

Can daily gifts be withdrawn?

No. Free-game and free-token rewards are promotional credits. The 5% reward applies only to your next eligible deposit.

FAIR PLAY

How do you keep games fair?

Questions, timers, turns, scores, and payouts are handled consistently for everyone. Every paid 10-10 and 5x5 game is recorded for private review, and repeated or late actions are not accepted.
